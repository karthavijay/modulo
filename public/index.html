Questions

Do people review the work?

Yes. A person on our team checks and approves the course design before any writing starts, and checks the finished course before it is shared with you. Your own experts should still check the facts, such as policies and procedures, before launch.

Will it work in our LMS?

Courses are delivered as SCORM 1.2 or SCORM 2004 packages, the formats almost every LMS accepts. Tell us your LMS and we test the package with you before launch.

Can we get Storyline or Rise files?

Not at the moment. We build courses in our own modern player, which works on phones and computers and is delivered as SCORM. We make any changes you need after launch.

Who can see our content?

Only our studio team, and Claude, the AI that helps write your course, which does not train on your content. We're happy to sign your NDA. Read more on our Security and privacy page.

What does it cost?

It depends on length and how interactive the course is. Send a brief and we reply with a proposal and a fixed price. A brief is free and commits you to nothing.
