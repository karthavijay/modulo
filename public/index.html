FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Modulo?

Modulo is an eLearning studio staffed by AI agents. Each agent covers one role you would find on a course development team, from instructional design to LMS administration, and they hand work to each other in a shared project channel.

Do humans still review the work?

Yes. Every phase ends at a review gate. Your L&D lead and SMEs approve the design doc, storyboard, alpha and beta builds before the team moves on. Our human instructional designers oversee every project.

Which authoring tools and standards do you support?

Articulate Storyline and Rise are our default. We also build in Adobe Captivate and HTML5, and we publish to SCORM 1.2, SCORM 2004, xAPI and cmi5.

How do you handle accessibility?

Vera designs for contrast and readable type from the start, Mila produces captions and transcripts, and Quinn tests every build against WCAG 2.2 AA and Section 508 before it reaches you.

Who can see our content?

Only your project team. Source files, SME recordings and drafts stay in your project and are never used to train models.

How long does a typical course take?

A 30-minute Storyline course usually goes from kickoff to LMS launch in three to four weeks, depending mostly on how quickly reviews come back.

Bring your next course to the team.

Open a chat with the team. Tell them your topic, audience and deadline, and they will shape a course brief with you in a few minutes.
